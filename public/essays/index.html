Some essays that might be of interest

Essays exploring the theory and practice of data-driven problem solving, process behavior charts, and quality improvement in manufacturing. Click the respective card to read.

The Needle & the Cannula

This essay is a case study in the application of a novel Statistical Process Control (SPC) methodology called network analysis. The method uses a grid of process behavior charts to facilitate uninterrupted visual reasoning.

Read The Needle & the Cannula

Introduction to Lithium-Ion Cells & Defects

This essay introduces lithium-ion cells by exploring their primary components, functions, and failure modes. Readers develop a broad understanding of the technology and how its components work together.

Read Intro to Cells & Defects

Using GD&T to Improve Manufacturing

This essay is a case study in using geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T) to improve an automated manufacturing process. Transitioning the vision system from a linear tolerance zone to a positional tolerance zone resulted in significant throughput improvements.

Read Using GD&T to Improve

What the Spec?

What are process limits, and how do they differ from specification limits? These distinctions play a critical role in improving quality and reducing costs. This essay presents operational definitions for both types of limits and explains the context required to put them to use.

Read What the Spec?

The Definition of Quality

This essay explores how the adherence-to-specifications approach to quality came to dominate manufacturing and explains why the Taguchi loss function provides a more robust and practical alternative for achieving world-class quality.

Read The Definition of Quality

Network Analysis: Advancing SPC

Modern manufacturing ecosystems produce data at an unprecedented scale, yet the way process behavior charts are analyzed has remained largely unchanged. Network analysis expands the utility of SPC to help make sense of that data more efficiently.

Read Network Analysis

The Taguchi Loss Function

This essay makes the case for replacing the traditional adherence-to-specifications, or goalpost, model of economic loss with the Taguchi loss function, a more realistic model of the economic costs associated with poor quality.
